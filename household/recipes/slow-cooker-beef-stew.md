# Slow-Cooker Beef Stew (GF)

_NEW 2026-09-19. Added for the week-of-9/20 clean week (Mon crockpot slot). Built GF from the ground up — **cornstarch, not flour**, so it fits the gluten-free stretch without tasting "adapted."_
_Serves 4 = dinner for the family + **leftover lunches** (Larissa) — stew keeps 4–5 days and reheats better than almost anything. Set-and-leave: ideal for the Mon dance night (girls out 4:45–8), ready whenever people eat._
_Why it earns a slot: hearty fall comfort, **veg-forward** (the broth carries a lot of vegetables painlessly), lean protein, and the crockpot removes the whole "cook at 7pm after drop-off" problem. Justin's red-meat variety night — occasional, not weekly._

## Ingredients (4 servings + leftovers)

### The stew
| Item | Quantity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Beef chuck / stew meat, 1½" cubes | ~2 lb | Trim visible fat. Chuck > pre-cut "stew meat" if you can — better marbling, less gristle. |
| Cornstarch | 3 Tbsp, divided | **The GF move** — 2 Tbsp to coat the beef, 1 Tbsp for the finishing slurry. (Sub arrowroot 1:1.) |
| Salt + pepper | to taste | |
| Olive oil | 1–2 Tbsp | For the sear |
| Onion, diced | 1 large | |
| Garlic, minced | 3 cloves | |
| Tomato paste | 2 Tbsp | Depth + a little body |
| Beef broth | ~4 cups | **Check GF label** (some contain wheat). |
| Worcestershire | 1 Tbsp | **Check GF** — US Lea & Perrins is GF; some brands aren't. Or sub coconut aminos / GF tamari. |
| Bay leaves | 2 | |
| Dried thyme | 1 tsp | + fresh at the end if you have it |

### The vegetables
| Item | Quantity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Carrots, 1" chunks | 4 | |
| Celery, 1" chunks | 3 stalks | |
| Yukon/red potatoes, 1½" chunks | ~1.5 lb | Skip or halve for a lower-carb pot; add extra carrot/turnip if so. |
| Frozen peas | 1 cup | **Stir in at the very end** (keeps them green, not gray). |

## Method

1. **Coat + sear (don't skip the sear — it's most of the flavor):** Pat beef dry, toss with **2 Tbsp cornstarch** + salt + pepper. Sear in olive oil over medium-high in batches, ~2 min/side, just to brown (not cook through). → crockpot.
2. **Quick fond build:** In the same pan, soften onion 2–3 min, add garlic + tomato paste, stir 1 min. Splash in a little broth and scrape up the browned bits → pour all of it into the crockpot.
3. **Load:** Add carrots, celery, potatoes, broth, Worcestershire, bay, thyme. Liquid should nearly cover — top with a little water if needed. Stir.
4. **Cook:** **LOW 7–8 hr** (or HIGH 4–5). Beef should shred at a fork.
5. **Thicken + finish:** Whisk **1 Tbsp cornstarch** into 2 Tbsp cold water → stir into the pot. Add **peas.** Lid off, HIGH 10–15 min until it coats a spoon. Pull bay leaves. Salt to taste; fresh thyme/parsley if you have it.

## Why it works for the program
- **GF by construction** — cornstarch coat + slurry does everything flour does; the only watch-outs are the broth and Worcestershire labels.
- **Veg-forward comfort** — a stew is one of the easiest ways to eat a lot of vegetables without it feeling like "eating your vegetables."
- **Crockpot = no 7pm cook** — set it in the AM, it's ready through the whole Monday dance stretch.
- **Cook-once-eat-twice** — reheats great; feeds Larissa's work lunches.

## Pro tips
- **The sear is the flavor.** Browning the cornstarch-coated beef builds the fond; a pale stew tastes flat.
- **Potato as the dial:** it's the main carb here. Half it (or swap in turnip/extra carrot) for a lighter pot on a clean week; keep it for the kids/comfort.
- **Peas last** — always at the end, or they turn army-green and mushy.
- **Day-2 is better** — flavors marry overnight; a great intentional-leftovers meal.
- Don't over-salt early — the broth reduces and concentrates over 8 hours.

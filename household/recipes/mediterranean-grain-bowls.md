# Mediterranean Grain Bowls

> **🔄 UPDATED 2026-10-06 — quantities now sized for 4 dinners + 1 leftover lunch portion** (Justin's tested build, v3). Quinoa only (no farro/couscous), more veg, double crispy chickpeas, 1.5× dressing. **Larissa's new favorite recipe.**

_NEW 2026-07-11. Sourced online (adapted from We Are Not Martha — see link). First cook: Tue 2026-07-14._
_Why it earns a slot: **plant-forward** (Larissa's preference), protein flexible (chickpeas carry it; chicken/shrimp/salmon on top), **meal-preps beautifully** for work lunches, and everything is composable so Justin can build his plate protein/veg-heavy and light on grain. Fits the clinician guidance (2026-10-05): plant protein ↑, low glycemic load (small quinoa scoop)._

## Ingredients (4 dinners + 1 leftover lunch)

### Grain base
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| **Quinoa**, rinsed (GF) | **1¼ cups dry** |
| Water | ~2¼–2½ cups |
| Olive oil | 1 tsp |
| Salt | to taste |

### Roasted vegetables
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| **Zucchini**, ¼" rounds | **2 medium** |
| Bell pepper, chunks | **1, optional** (often added) |
| Cherry tomatoes | 1 pint |
| Red onion, wedges | 1 small |
| Olive oil | ~3 Tbsp (scaled to the larger veg load) |
| Salt / pepper / smoked paprika | ¾ tsp / ¼ tsp / ½ tsp |

### Crispy chickpeas — DOUBLED (Larissa likes the leftovers)
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Chickpeas, drained + dried well | **2 cans (15 oz each)** |
| Olive oil | 2 Tbsp |
| Cumin / coriander / salt | 2 tsp / 1 tsp / ½ tsp |

### Lemon-tahini dressing — 1.5×
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Tahini | **6 Tbsp** (⅜ cup) |
| Lemon zest | **1½ Tbsp** |
| Lemon juice | **4½ Tbsp** (¼ cup + 1½ tsp) |
| Garlic, grated | 1–2 cloves |
| Salt | heaping ¼ tsp |
| Warm water | **6 Tbsp** (+ more to thin) |

### Protein on top + toppings
- **Protein (pick one):** grilled/sautéed **chicken** (v2 standard), **shrimp** (v2.1 — D1-praised), or **salmon**. Optional lemon-oregano chicken marinade: 1½ lb chicken breast in ¼ cup lemon juice + 1 Tbsp zest + 3 Tbsp olive oil + 3 cloves garlic + 2 tsp oregano + 1 tsp salt + ½ tsp paprika (30 min–12 hr), seared 4–5 min/side to 165°F, rested + sliced.
- Diced cucumber, kalamata olives, crumbled feta, chopped parsley + mint.

## Method

1. **Roast (425°F), staggered trays:** veg on one sheet, chickpeas on another (toss each per above). **Chickpeas ~20 min; veg ~25–30 min**, stirring halfway — pull the chickpeas first. On busy nights (e.g., Monday dance), roast both in the afternoon and just reheat/assemble at dinner.
2. **Quinoa:** rinse well; bring quinoa + water + 1 tsp oil + salt to a boil, cover, simmer on low **~15 min** until the water's absorbed; rest 5 min covered, fluff. (1¼ cups dry → ~4 cups cooked.)
3. **Dressing:** whisk everything smooth; thin with warm water to a drizzle.
4. **Protein:** cook the chicken/shrimp/salmon while the trays roast.
5. **Assemble:** quinoa → roasted veg + crispy chickpeas + protein → cucumber/olives/feta → drizzle tahini → parsley + mint.

## Why it works for the program
- **Plant-forward + protein-flexible** — double chickpeas + an animal protein on top = satisfying for everyone.
- **Cook-once-eat-twice** — components keep 4–5 days; the leftover portion is Larissa's work lunch.
- **Composable = built-in plate flex** — Justin controls his own grain-vs-protein ratio without cooking a separate meal.

## Pro tips
- **Dry the chickpeas thoroughly** before roasting — the single biggest lever on crispiness.
- **Stagger the roast** — chickpeas crisp faster than the veg.
- **Dress-to-order for leftovers** — keep tahini separate so the grains/veg don't go soggy.
- **Quinoa is the default grain** (GF + lower-wheat) — the original farro/couscous build is retired.

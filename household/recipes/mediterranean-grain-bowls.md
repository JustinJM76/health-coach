# Mediterranean Grain Bowls

_NEW 2026-07-11. Sourced online (adapted from We Are Not Martha — see link). First cook: Tue 2026-07-14._
_Serves 4 = dinner for 2 adults + **leftover lunches** (Larissa's request). Components keep separately 4–5 days._
_Why it earns a slot: **plant-forward** (Larissa's preference), protein flexible (chickpeas carry it; chicken optional), **meal-preps beautifully** for work lunches, and everything is composable so Justin can build his plate protein/veg-heavy and light on grain._

## Ingredients (4 servings)

### Grain base
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Farro, rinsed | ¾ cup |
| Pearled couscous (or all farro, or quinoa for GF) | ¾ cup |
| Olive oil | ~1 Tbsp + 1 tsp |
| Salt | to taste |

### Roasted vegetables
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Zucchini, ¼" rounds | 1 medium |
| Cherry tomatoes | 1 pint |
| Red onion, wedges | 1 small |
| Olive oil | 2 Tbsp |
| Salt / pepper / smoked paprika | ½ tsp / ¼ tsp / ¼ tsp |

### Crispy chickpeas (the plant-protein engine)
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Chickpeas, drained + dried well | 1 can (15 oz) |
| Olive oil | 1 Tbsp |
| Cumin / coriander / salt | 1 tsp / ½ tsp / ¼ tsp |

### Lemon-tahini dressing
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Tahini | ¼ cup |
| Lemon zest | 1 Tbsp |
| Lemon juice | 3 Tbsp |
| Garlic, grated | 1 clove |
| Salt | ¼ tsp |
| Warm water | ¼ cup (+ more to thin) |

### Optional protein + toppings
- **Lemon-oregano chicken** (optional, for extra protein): 1½ lb chicken breast marinated in ¼ cup lemon juice + 1 Tbsp zest + 3 Tbsp olive oil + 3 cloves garlic + 2 tsp oregano + 1 tsp salt + ½ tsp paprika (30 min–12 hr), seared 4–5 min/side to 165°F, rested + sliced.
- Diced cucumber, kalamata olives, crumbled feta, chopped parsley + mint.

## Method

1. **Roast (425°F):** veg on one sheet, chickpeas on another (toss each per above). **20–25 min**, stirring halfway — veg caramelized, chickpeas crisping.
2. **Grains:** farro → simmer in salted water + 1 tsp oil ~20–25 min, rest 5, fluff. Couscous → 1¼ cup salted water + 1 Tbsp oil, boil, add couscous, cover low 8–10 min, fluff.
3. **Dressing:** whisk everything smooth; thin with warm water to a drizzle.
4. **(Optional) chicken:** sear the marinated breast, rest, slice.
5. **Assemble:** grains → roasted veg + crispy chickpeas (+ chicken) → cucumber/olives/feta → drizzle tahini → parsley + mint.

## Plates

| Plate | Build |
|---|---|
| **Family / Larissa** | Full bowl; pack extras as next-day lunch (dress just before eating). |
| **Justin** | **Load chickpeas + veg (+ chicken if made), go light on the grain** (½ the grain scoop). Protein comes from chickpeas + optional chicken + tahini; the grain is a garnish, not the base. |

## Why it works for the program
- **Plant-forward + protein-flexible** — chickpeas alone make it satisfying; add chicken on a heavier-training day.
- **Cook-once-eat-twice** — keeps 4–5 days; ideal for Larissa's work lunches (store components separate, dress at the table).
- **Composable = built-in plate flex** — Justin controls his own grain-vs-protein ratio without cooking a separate meal.

## Pro tips
- **Dry the chickpeas thoroughly** before roasting — the single biggest lever on crispiness.
- **Dress-to-order for leftovers** — keep tahini separate so the grains/veg don't go soggy.
- Sub **quinoa** for the farro/couscous to make it gluten-free (relevant to Justin's wheat-stiffness hypothesis — a low-wheat build of this bowl is easy).

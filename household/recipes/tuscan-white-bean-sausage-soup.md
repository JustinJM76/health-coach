# Tuscan White Bean & Sausage Soup (with kale)

_NEW 2026-10-03. Added as Justin's "new recipe to try" — seasonal (first real soup of the fall). First cook planned Wed 2026-10-07._
_Serves 4 = dinner for the family + **leftover lunches** (Larissa) — soup keeps 4–5 days and is better day 2._
_Total time: ~35–40 min stovetop (or crockpot — see variant). One pot._
_Why it earns a slot: **protein + big vegetable volume** (sausage + white beans carry the protein; kale + mirepoix carry the volume) = a satisfying big bowl at low calorie density — ideal for Justin's volumetric plate. Plant-leaning (white beans + greens), GF by construction (check the sausage + broth labels), and a natural crockpot/game-night meal._

## Ingredients (4 servings + leftovers)

| Item | Quantity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Lean Italian chicken or turkey sausage** | ~1 lb | Casings removed (crumble) or sliced. **Poultry, not pork** — keeps it lean. |
| Olive oil | 1 Tbsp | |
| Yellow onion, diced | 1 medium | |
| Carrots, diced | 3 | |
| Celery, diced | 2 stalks | |
| Garlic, minced | 3–4 cloves | |
| Cannellini / white beans, drained + rinsed | 2 cans (15 oz) | The plant-protein + body |
| Diced tomatoes (canned) | 1 can (14 oz) | Optional; or all broth for a lighter color |
| **Low-sodium** chicken broth | ~6 cups | Low-sodium matters — the sausage + parm already bring salt |
| Kale, stemmed + chopped | 1 bunch (~4 cups) | Or baby kale / spinach (spinach wilts faster) |
| Italian seasoning (or thyme + rosemary) | 1–2 tsp | + 1 bay leaf |
| Parmesan rind | 1, optional | Simmer it in — big depth for free |
| Red pepper flakes | to taste | **Justin's heat lever** — add at his bowl |
| Salt + pepper | to taste | Go easy on salt until the end |
| Lemon juice or splash of vinegar | to finish | Brightens the whole pot |
| Grated parmesan | to serve | |

## Method (stovetop)

1. **Brown the sausage** in the olive oil over medium-high, breaking it up, ~5–6 min until it has color. (Leave it in the pot.)
2. **Soften the mirepoix:** add onion, carrot, celery to the sausage; cook 5–7 min until soft. Add garlic, stir 1 min.
3. **Build the soup:** add broth, tomatoes, beans, Italian seasoning, bay leaf, and the parm rind if using. Bring to a simmer, **15–20 min** (flavors marry; a few beans can be mashed against the side to thicken).
4. **Kale:** stir in, simmer **5 min** until tender (spinach: 2 min).
5. **Finish:** pull the bay + parm rind. Add a squeeze of lemon or splash of vinegar, salt + pepper to taste. Serve with grated parm (+ red pepper flakes at Justin's bowl).

### Crockpot variant (game-night / set-and-leave)
Brown the sausage + sauté the aromatics first (worth the 8 min), then add everything **except kale** → LOW 6–7 hr. Stir kale in for the last 20–30 min.

## Why it works for the program
- **Protein + volume:** sausage + white beans hit the protein target; mirepoix + kale + broth make it a *big* low-cal-dense bowl — satiety without the calories.
- **Plant-leaning** (white beans + greens) — hits the plant-forward cadence without going meatless.
- **GF by construction** — just verify the sausage and broth labels.
- **Cook-once-eat-twice** — ideal for Larissa's work lunches; improves overnight.
- **Fall one-pot** — minimal cleanup, crockpot-able for the hectic dance/game nights.

## Pro tips
- **Parm rind = free depth.** Keep a bag of rinds in the freezer for exactly this.
- **Low-sodium broth** — the sausage and parmesan are salty; you can always add salt at the end, you can't take it out.
- **Don't overcook the kale** — 5 min keeps it green and toothsome; longer turns it drab.
- **Acid at the end matters** — a squeeze of lemon or splash of vinegar lifts the whole pot out of "flat."
- **Thicken naturally** — mash a ladle of beans against the side instead of adding starch.

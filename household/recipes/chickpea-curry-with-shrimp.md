# Chickpea curry with side of shrimp

_First cooked: 2026-05-20 (Wednesday) — to be evaluated_
_Rotation: weekly plant-forward slot (hits the ≥1 plant-forward dinner/week target)_
_Serves: family of 4_
_Total time: ~25 min_

## Why this is a good rotation pick

- **Hits the plant-forward dinner cadence** (chickpeas as centerpiece, not garnish)
- **Hybrid plate works for Justin** — chickpeas + shrimp together get him to ~30g protein on the plate without making it a meat-heavy meal
- **Family-friendly + Justin spicy variant** — base curry is mild; Justin adds heat at the plate (chili crisp, sriracha, or extra red pepper flakes)
- **Pantry + freezer friendly** — chickpeas, coconut milk, tomatoes, spices are all shelf-stable; shrimp lives in the freezer
- **Crockpot-day alternative** — can be made on weeknights when crockpot isn't running

## Ingredients (4 servings)

### Curry base
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Chickpeas, drained + rinsed | 2 cans (15 oz each) |
| Full-fat coconut milk | 1 can (14 oz) |
| Diced tomatoes (canned) | 1 can (14 oz) |
| Yellow onion, diced | 1 medium |
| Garlic, minced | 3–4 cloves |
| Fresh ginger, grated | 1 Tbsp (or 1 tsp ground) |
| Curry paste (red or green) **OR** curry powder + garam masala | 2–3 Tbsp paste **OR** 2 Tbsp powder + 1 tsp garam masala |
| Neutral oil | 1 Tbsp |
| Salt | to taste (start with 1 tsp) |
| Baby spinach (optional) | 1–2 cups |

### Shrimp side
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Shrimp, peeled + thawed + patted dry | 1 lb |
| Garlic, minced | 2 cloves |
| Curry powder | 1 tsp |
| Salt | ½ tsp |
| Neutral oil | 1 tsp |

### Serving + toppings
- Cooked rice (jasmine or basmati) **or** naan, warmed
- Lime wedges
- Fresh cilantro, chopped
- _Justin's heat lever:_ chili crisp, sriracha, or extra red pepper flakes

## Method

### Curry
1. Heat 1 Tbsp oil in a large saucepan or Dutch oven over medium heat.
2. **Sauté onion 3–4 min** until soft and translucent.
3. Add garlic + ginger, stir 1 min until fragrant.
4. Add curry paste (or curry powder + garam masala). **Toast 30 sec** — this wakes up the spices and is the difference between flat and full-flavored curry. Don't skip.
5. Add diced tomatoes, coconut milk, chickpeas, salt. Stir.
6. **Simmer 15–20 min** uncovered, stirring occasionally. Sauce should thicken slightly.
7. If using spinach, stir in last 2 min until wilted.
8. Taste, adjust salt + add lime juice if it tastes flat.

### Shrimp (parallel, last 5 min)
1. Toss shrimp with garlic + curry powder + salt + 1 tsp oil.
2. High-heat pan **or** air fryer 400°F — 3–4 min total until pink. Don't overcook (they go from done to rubbery in 30 sec).

### Rice (start first; ~20 min cook)
- ½ cup cooked rice per Justin's plate, more for family.

## Pro tips

- **Toast the curry paste/powder for 30 sec before adding liquids.** Single most important step for flavor depth.
- **Full-fat coconut milk, not light.** Light coconut milk is watered down; the fat does the satiety + flavor work.
- **Don't overcook the shrimp.** Pull at 3 min and check — they should be just-pink, not curled tight.
- **Acid at the table matters.** Lime brightens the curry; without it the dish tastes muddy.
- **Doubles + freezes well.** Curry alone (no shrimp) freezes for 2–3 months. Make a double batch for a freezer stash.

## Variations

- **Vegetarian:** skip the shrimp; add a second can of chickpeas or 1 cup of paneer / firm tofu / cashews for protein. Plant-only plate.
- **Chicken version:** dice 1 lb chicken thigh, sear before the onions, set aside, add back at simmer step.
- **Red lentils instead of chickpeas:** dial up the liquid (1 extra cup broth/water), cook 25–30 min. Different texture, same plant-protein angle.
- **Spinach → kale:** add chopped kale 5 min before serving (takes longer than spinach to soften).

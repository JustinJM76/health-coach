# Italian-style beef meatballs from scratch

_First cooked: 2026-05-01 (Friday spaghetti night)_
_Rotation: Friday flex slot — when family wants spaghetti & meatballs_
_Serves: family of 4 + leftovers (~20 meatballs)_

## Ingredients

### Meatballs
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Ground beef (85/15) | 1.75 lb |
| Large egg | 1 |
| Breadcrumbs (panko or Italian) | ½ cup |
| Milk | ¼ cup |
| Grated parmesan | ¼ cup |
| Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| Fresh parsley, chopped | 2 Tbsp (or 2 tsp dried) |
| Kosher salt | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | ½ tsp |
| _Spicy variant:_ red pepper flakes | ½ tsp |
| _Optional moisture booster:_ grated yellow onion (squeezed dry) | ¼ cup |

### Sauce
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Marinara, jarred (Rao's, Victoria, etc.) | 1 jar (~24 oz) |
| Olive oil | 1 Tbsp (optional, for blooming) |
| Garlic, minced (extra) | 2 cloves (optional) |
| _Spicy:_ red pepper flakes | pinch |

## Method

1. **Soak breadcrumbs in milk** in a large mixing bowl, 5 min. (Panade — the difference between tender and tough meatballs. Don't skip.)
2. **Add beef, egg, parmesan, garlic, parsley, salt, pepper, optional add-ins.**
3. **Mix gently with hands until just combined.** Do NOT overmix — overworked meatballs get tough. Stop the moment everything is incorporated.
4. **Roll into ~1.25 oz balls** (slightly larger than a golf ball). Yields ~20. Small ice cream scoop helps consistency. Place on parchment-lined sheet pan.
5. **Bake at 400°F for 18–20 min** until internal **160°F.** Browned on top.
6. **Warm sauce while baking.** Optional flavor boost: heat olive oil, sizzle minced garlic 30 sec, add jar marinara, simmer 5 min.
7. **Transfer baked meatballs to the sauce. Simmer 10 min** before serving. Spoon over pasta.

|---|---|
| **Family** | 1.5–2 cups cooked spaghetti, 3–4 meatballs, generous sauce, parmesan, garlic bread |
| **Justin** | ½–¾ cup spaghetti, **5–6 meatballs** (the protein is the star), generous sauce, **big side salad**, skip or 1 small slice garlic bread |

Justin's plate macros: ~40 g protein, ~50 g carbs, ~600 cal.

## Pro tips

- **Don't overmix.** Single biggest mistake. Mix to just-combined and stop.
- **Don't skip the panade.** Breadcrumbs + milk = tender. Breadcrumbs alone = dense.
- **Bake, don't pan-fry, for this volume.** Pan-frying 20 meatballs = 4 batches; oven = 1 batch.
- **Double and freeze.** Make 40, eat 20, freeze the rest single-layer on a tray, then bag them. Future Friday spaghetti = 25 min from freezer to plate. Reheat: thaw in fridge overnight, then simmer in sauce 15 min, OR bake from frozen at 350°F for 25–30 min.

## Why this works for the program

- **Beef (vs. frozen):** real ground beef + minimal filler ≈ frozen turkey nutritionally. Better flavor, better texture, comparable health profile.
- **Justin's plate weighted toward protein, not pasta** — the meatballs are the centerpiece, pasta is the side.
- **Big salad alongside** keeps fiber + volume up without piling on refined carbs.
- **Spicy variant** uses Justin's go-to flavor lever; family version skips the heat.

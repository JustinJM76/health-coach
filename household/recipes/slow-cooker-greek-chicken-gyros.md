# Slow Cooker Greek Chicken Gyros + Tzatziki

_NEW 2026-06-06. Sourced online (adapted from The Recipe Critic — see link). First cook: Tue 2026-06-09._
_Rotation: crockpot slot / quick-dinner slot. Set in the AM, ready whenever the evening lands — ideal for busy dance-rehearsal nights._
_Serves: family of 4 + lunch leftovers_
_Why it earns a slot: dump-and-go crockpot (zero evening cook time), **served cool/assembled — not a hot stew, so it works in hot weather**, lean breast = high protein, tzatziki adds a Greek-yogurt protein bonus, and Justin's spicy lever layers at the plate._

## Ingredients (4 servings + leftovers)

### Chicken (crockpot)
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Boneless skinless chicken breast | 2 lb |
| Yellow onion, diced | 1 cup (~1 medium) |
| Garlic, minced | 2 tsp |
| Dried oregano | 1 tsp |
| Salt | ½ tsp |
| Black pepper | ½ tsp |
| Olive oil | 3 Tbsp |
| Red wine vinegar | 1 Tbsp |
| Lemon juice | 2 Tbsp |
| Water | ⅓ cup |

### Tzatziki
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Plain Greek yogurt | 2 cups |
| Cucumber, finely grated (then squeezed dry) | 1 cup |
| Garlic, minced | 2 tsp |
| White wine vinegar | 1 Tbsp |
| Dried dill | 1 tsp |
| Dried oregano | 1 tsp |
| Lemon juice | 2 Tbsp |
| Extra-virgin olive oil | 1 Tbsp |
| Salt + pepper | to taste |

### Assembly / toppings
- Warm pita or flatbread (pocketless)
- Shredded romaine, diced tomato, diced cucumber, diced red onion
- Feta crumbles, Kalamata olives
- _Justin's heat lever:_ harissa, red pepper flakes, or hot sauce

## Method

1. **Crockpot (AM):** spray the insert. Add chicken. Whisk the rest of the chicken ingredients in a bowl, pour over. Cook **LOW 6–8 hr** or **HIGH 4–6 hr.**
2. **Tzatziki (anytime that day):** grate the cucumber and **squeeze it dry in a towel** (skip this and it goes watery). Mix everything, finish with the olive oil, chill ≥30 min.
3. **Serve:** shred the chicken in the juices. Build pitas/bowls with chicken + tzatziki + toppings.

## Plates

| Plate | Build |
|---|---|
| **Family** | Warm pita + chicken + tzatziki + veg + feta/olives |
| **Justin** | **Bowl, not pita** (or one small pita) — chicken over a big bed of romaine/cucumber/tomato + tzatziki + feta + olives + heat. Double the salad, skip/limit the bread. |

**Justin's plate protein:** lean breast (~40 g) + Greek-yogurt tzatziki (~5–8 g) — easily hits target without the bread.

## Why it works for the program

- **Zero evening cook time** — the whole point on a dance-rehearsal week.
- **Hot-weather friendly** — assembled cool, not a heavy hot stew (unlike chili).
- **Cook-once-eat-twice** — shredded chicken + tzatziki make excellent next-day lunch bowls (Larissa-friendly too).
- **Bowl format = Justin's plate-composition flex built in** — swap pita for greens, keep the protein.
- **Spicy lever at the plate** — family gets it mild, Justin layers heat.

## Pro tips

- **Squeeze the grated cucumber dry** before it goes in the tzatziki — the single most important step for texture.
- **Don't overcook the breast** — at HIGH it can dry past ~6 hr; LOW 6–8 is the more forgiving setting for a WFH day.
- **Tzatziki is better made a few hours ahead** — the garlic and dill bloom.
- Leftover chicken holds 3–4 days; tzatziki ~4 days.

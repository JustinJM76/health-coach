# Justin's Turkey Chili (crockpot)

_Added 2026-08-29 — **Justin's own long-standing recipe** (from memory; spice quantities are approximate, he does it by feel). First logged cook: Tue 2026-09-01._
_Rotation: crockpot slot, alternating with white chicken chili + pulled chicken. Set in AM, ready by dinner — ideal for hectic evenings (open house, dance nights)._
_Serves: family of 4 + leftovers (leftovers are the plan — e.g., feeds a later leftover night)._
_**Mild by design** — the heat goes on at the table (Justin's spicy lever)._

## Ingredients

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Ground turkey | 1–1.5 lb |
| Yellow onion, diced | 2 large |
| Bell peppers (mix of colors), diced | 3 |
| Black beans | 2 cans |
| Fire-roasted diced tomatoes | 2 cans |
| Brown rice, uncooked | 1 cup |
| Garlic, minced | 4 cloves |
| Kosher salt | ~2 tsp |
| Black pepper, fresh ground | liberal |
| Chili powder | ~4–6 Tbsp |
| Cumin | ~2 Tbsp |
| Coriander | ~1 Tbsp |
| Water | 1 cup |
| _Optional:_ jalapeño, diced | 1–2 |

## Method

1. **Cook the rice** per package (1 cup brown rice : 2 cups water, simmer until done).
2. **Brown the turkey** in a skillet with a little olive oil → move to the crockpot.
3. **Sauté the onions** with a little salt until translucent + just starting to color → move to the crockpot.
4. **Add everything else** (peppers, beans, tomatoes, cooked rice, garlic, spices, water, optional jalapeño) to the crockpot.
5. **Cook on LOW for 8 hours.**

## Serve
Mild base — set out **hot sauce, diced jalapeños, cornbread** for the table.

## Plates

| Plate | Build |
|---|---|
| **Family** | Bowl + cornbread + toppings of choice. |
| **Justin** | Big bowl, **turkey + beans forward**; the brown rice is cooked in, so portion by bowl size (or ladle over a bed of greens to bulk the veg and stretch the same scoop). His spicy variant: jalapeño + hot sauce. Skip the cornbread. |

## Why it works for the program
- **Crockpot set-and-forget** — set in the AM, ready whenever the evening lands (open-house / dance nights).
- **Cook-once-eat-twice** — big yield → leftovers are the plan (great next-day lunch or a leftover dinner).
- **Turkey + beans = lean protein + fiber + plant-protein bonus;** brown rice > white as the built-in starch.
- **Mild base + heat-at-the-table** — family gets it gentle, Justin layers his spicy lever. No separate cooking.

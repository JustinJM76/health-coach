# Crockpot white chicken chili

_First planned: 2026-05-13 (Wednesday) — to be cooked_
_Rotation: weekly crockpot slot, alternating with red turkey-rice chili and pulled chicken_
_Serves: family of 4 + leftovers_

## Why this is a good rotation pick

- **Different from red turkey chili** — different flavor profile (mild green chile + cumin vs. tomato + sausage), different beans (cannellini vs. kidney), different finishing herbs (cilantro + lime vs. just heat)
- **Plant-protein bonus** — beans are a real protein contributor (~7g per ½ cup), counts toward the weekly plant-forward cadence
- **Family-friendly default + Justin spicy variant** — base recipe is mild; Justin adjusts at the bowl with jalapeños, hot sauce, lime
- **Crockpot set-and-forget** — set in AM, ready by dinner. Great for hectic weekdays.

## Ingredients

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Boneless skinless chicken breasts | 1.5 lb (whole — shred at end) |
| Cannellini OR navy beans, drained + rinsed | 2 cans (15 oz each) |
| Corn (canned drained or frozen) | 1 cup |
| Diced green chiles (mild) | 1 can (4 oz) |
| Yellow onion, diced | 1 medium |
| Garlic, minced | 4 cloves |
| Chicken broth | 4 cups |
| Cumin | 2 tsp |
| Dried oregano | 1 tsp |
| Coriander | 1 tsp |
| Salt | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | ½ tsp |
| _Spicy variant for Justin's bowl:_ jalapeño, chopped, OR 1 chipotle in adobo | 1 |
| _"Creamy" finish (optional, family preference):_ half-and-half OR cream cheese | ½ cup OR 4 oz |

## Method

1. **Add everything except the chicken to the crockpot** in the morning. Stir.
2. **Nest the chicken breasts in the liquid** (don't dice — they'll shred easily later).
3. **Crockpot on LOW for 6–8 hours** OR HIGH for 3–4 hours.
4. **Last 30 min: shred chicken** with two forks directly in the crockpot, stir back in.
5. **For thicker / creamier version:** mash some of the beans against the side of the crockpot with a wooden spoon. Optional: stir in half-and-half or cream cheese during the last 30 min until melted.
6. **Taste and adjust** — more salt, more cumin, more lime juice if it tastes flat.

## Toppings (set out for the table)

- Fresh cilantro, chopped
- Lime wedges
- Sliced jalapeños (fresh or pickled)
- Hot sauce (Cholula, Frank's, etc.)
- Shredded Monterey jack or cheddar (family)
- Sour cream (family)
- Crushed tortilla chips (family)
- Diced avocado (optional, modest portion for Justin)

## Plates

| Plate | Build |
|---|---|
| **Family** | Big bowl + cheese + sour cream + chips, optional cornbread or tortillas on the side |
| **Justin** | Big bowl + cilantro + lime + jalapeños + hot sauce + small spoon of avocado; skip cheese/sour cream/chips |

Justin's bowl: ~30g protein, ~350 cal, satisfying. The beans + chicken combo carries through the evening cleanly.

## Why this works for the program

- **Plant + animal protein in one pot** — beans add fiber + plant protein; chicken anchors animal protein. Hits both the protein target and the wife's plant-protein request.
- **Spicy lever for Justin** without forcing the family — toppings let everyone adjust at the bowl
- **Holds beautifully in the crockpot** on warm — perfect for staggered family eating times
- **Leftovers improve overnight** as flavors meld; great next-day lunch

## Pro tips

- **Don't dice the chicken before cooking.** Whole breasts shred far better than chopped chicken. Trust the process.
- **Don't skip the lime at the table.** Acid is what makes white chili sing — without it, it tastes flat regardless of seasoning.
- **The "creamy" version isn't healthier** — it adds saturated fat. Add it only if family prefers; Justin's bowl can come from the pre-cream portion.
- **Doubles well + freezes well.** If you want a freezer stash, double the recipe and freeze in quart containers for future hectic weeks.

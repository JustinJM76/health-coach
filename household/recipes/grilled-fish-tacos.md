# Grilled Fish Tacos (mahi)

_Added 2026-07-15. First cook: Wed 2026-07-15 (mahi). Weber Searwood / any hot grill._
_Serves 2 + leftovers (fish + slaw hold for next-day lunch bowls)._
_Why it earns a slot: lean high-protein fish, fast (~10 min on the grill), summer-light, and a kids-skip-it "just us" pick. Bowl format = built-in plate flex for Justin._

## Ingredients (2 + leftovers)

### Fish + rub
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Mahi-mahi fillets (or cod/snapper) | ~1 lb |
| Olive oil | 1–2 Tbsp |
| Chili powder | 2 tsp |
| Cumin | 1 tsp |
| Smoked paprika | 1 tsp |
| Garlic powder | ½ tsp |
| Salt / pepper | ¾ tsp / ¼ tsp |
| Lime zest + a squeeze of juice | 1 lime |

### Quick slaw
- ½ small cabbage (green and/or purple), shredded
- Juice of 1 lime, pinch of salt, splash of olive oil
- Optional: cilantro, thin-sliced jalapeño

### Lime crema (Greek-yogurt = protein bump)
- ½ cup plain Greek yogurt (5%)
- Juice + zest of ½ lime, pinch of salt
- Optional: 1 tsp adobo/chipotle or hot sauce (Justin's heat lever)

### Assembly
- Corn tortillas (street size), warmed/charred on the grill
- Avocado, cilantro, lime wedges, hot sauce

## Method

1. **Prep fish:** pat mahi very dry. Mix the rub spices; rub the fish with a little oil, then the spice mix + lime zest. Rest 10–15 min.
2. **Grate + slaw + crema:** toss slaw ingredients; whisk the crema. (Both can be made ahead / kept for leftovers.)
3. **Hot, clean, oiled grates.** Preheat to medium-high. Oil the grates well (mahi will stick if they're cold/dry).
4. **Grill ~3–4 min per side** for ~1" fillets. **Don't move it too early** — let it release on its own, then flip once. Pull at **~135–140°F / when it just flakes** (carryover finishes it; overcooking dries mahi fast).
5. **Char the tortillas** ~15 sec/side on the grill.
6. **Flake the fish**, build tacos: tortilla → slaw → fish → crema → avocado + cilantro + lime + hot sauce.

## Plates

| Plate | Build |
|---|---|
| **Family / Larissa** | 2–3 tacos, full toppings. |
| **Justin** | **Double the slaw, 2 street-size tortillas** (or **bowl it** over slaw/greens, skip tortillas). Fish + avocado + Greek-yogurt crema carry the protein/fat; keep it veg-forward. |

## Pro tips
- **Dry fish + hot, oiled grates = no sticking + no falling apart.** The #1 failure mode.
- **Firm fish (mahi/snapper) grills far better than cod** (which flakes apart) — for cod, use a foil sheet or fish basket.
- **Pull it early** — mahi goes from perfect to dry in a minute; ~140°F and carryover does the rest.
- **Greek-yogurt crema** swaps in for sour cream and adds protein — on-brand and no one notices.
- Slaw + fish keep well → next-day **taco bowls** for lunch.

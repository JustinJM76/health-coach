# Shrimp stir-fry

_First cooked: 2026-05-12 (Tuesday) — family hit_
_Rotation: Tuesday quick-dinner slot (alternates with pan-seared chicken, salmon)_
_Serves: family of 4_
_Total time: ~20 min_

## Why this is a good rotation pick

- **Fast** — 20 min start to plate, fits Tuesday's tight evening window
- **Lean protein density** — 1.5 lb shrimp = ~140g protein across the family; ~35g/serving for Justin
- **Family-friendly + Justin spicy variant** — base sauce is mild; chili crisp / sriracha drizzled on Justin's plate, not in the pan
- **Vegetable vehicle** — 2 lb bag of stir-fry veg + the sauce = real volume on the plate
- **Frozen-pantry friendly** — both shrimp and veg can live in the freezer indefinitely; no shopping urgency

## Ingredients (4 servings)

**Protein + veg:**
- 1.5 lb raw shrimp (frozen, peeled & deveined)
- 2 lb bag frozen stir-fry vegetables
- 2 cups cooked rice (~½ cup per plate for Justin; more for kids)

**Sauce:**
- 5–6 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tsp grated ginger (or 1 tsp ground)
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp rice vinegar
- 2 tsp honey or brown sugar
- 2 tsp sesame oil (added off heat at the end)
- Optional: 2 tsp cornstarch + 2 tbsp water slurry to thicken

**Justin's at-plate add-ons:**
- Chili crisp or sriracha (heat lever)
- Extra lime if available

## Method

1. **Thaw shrimp + veg under cold running water.** Drain HARD. Pat both dry with paper towels — wet stir-fry steams instead of sears.
2. **Cook rice** (rice cooker or stovetop) while prep happens.
3. **Whisk sauce** (everything except sesame oil and chili crisp) in a small bowl. Set aside.
4. **High heat, neutral oil in pan/wok.** If pan is small, cook in two batches; piling 2 lb of veg in one pan tanks the heat.
5. **Veg first**, 3–4 min, stir occasionally, let them sit and brown between stirs. Push to one side.
6. **Shrimp last**, 1 min per side. They go from translucent to pink fast — pull as soon as they're opaque.
7. **Sauce in at the end**, 30–60 sec, stir to coat everything.
8. **Off heat, drizzle sesame oil.** Plate.
9. **At the table:** chili crisp / sriracha on Justin's plate. Kids decide their own heat.

## Variations worth trying

- **Chicken instead of shrimp** — bite-sized thigh pieces, sear ~3 min/side before the veg
- **Beef** — thin-sliced sirloin, marinated 10 min in 1 tbsp soy + 1 tsp cornstarch
- **Tofu** (plant-forward variant) — extra-firm, pressed, cubed, pan-fried separately to crisp, added at the end with the sauce

## Sodium note

Soy sauce is the dominant sodium source. Low-sodium soy keeps a single serving in a reasonable range. Expect a small AM scale bump the next morning (water, not fat) — read it that way.

# Greek lemon chicken sheet pan + cucumber-tomato salad

_First cooked: 2026-04-30 (Thursday "real dinner" slot) — confirmed family hit_
_Rotation: Thursday "real dinner" slot, every other week_
_Serves: family of 4 + lunch leftovers_

## Ingredients

### Chicken + marinade
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Boneless skinless chicken thighs | 2 lb |
| Olive oil | ¼ cup |
| Lemon (juice + zest) | 1 large |
| Garlic, minced | 4 cloves (or 1 tsp powder) |
| Dried oregano | 1 Tbsp |
| Dried thyme | 1 tsp |
| Salt | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | ½ tsp |
| _Spicy variant:_ red pepper flakes | ½ tsp |

### Roasted potatoes (family)
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Yukon gold potatoes, 1-inch wedges | 1.5 lb |
| Olive oil | 2 Tbsp |
| Salt | ½ tsp |
| Oregano | ½ tsp |

### Cucumber-tomato salad
| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Cucumbers, diced | 2 |
| Roma/vine tomatoes, diced | 4 (or 2 cups cherry, halved) |
| Red onion, thinly sliced | ¼, optional |
| Feta crumbles | ½ cup |
| Pitted Kalamata olives | ¼ cup |
| Olive oil | 2 Tbsp |
| Red wine vinegar (or lemon juice) | 1 Tbsp |
| Oregano | ½ tsp |
| Salt + pepper | to taste |
| _Optional:_ canned chickpeas, rinsed | 1 can |

## Method

1. **Marinate chicken (~10 min).** Whisk marinade ingredients, add chicken, toss to coat. Let sit on counter while oven preheats.
2. **Preheat oven 425°F.** Toss potato wedges with olive oil + salt + oregano. Spread on sheet pan.
3. **Roast potatoes for 10 min head start.**
4. **Add chicken to pan** (single layer; second pan if crowded). Roast another 22–25 min until chicken hits **165°F internal.**
5. **Make salad while it cooks.** Combine vegetables/feta/olives, whisk dressing, toss together. Add chickpeas if using.
6. **Pull chicken at 165°F. Rest 3 min** before serving.

## Plates

|---|---|
| **Family** | 1–2 thighs, generous potatoes, salad |
| **Justin** | 2 thighs (~40 g protein), **double salad**, tiny/skip potatoes |

## Why it works

- ~40 g protein on Justin's plate
- Cucumber-tomato salad = real meal-companion, not token side
- Mediterranean flavor template (lemon + oregano + olive oil) pairs well with hot sauce
- Skipping potatoes on Justin's plate keeps refined-carb low while family still gets starch
- **Eat salad tonight** — gets watery as leftovers. Chicken + potatoes hold fine for next-day lunch.

## Pro tips

- **Don't over-marinate.** 10–30 min is plenty for thighs. >1 hour and lemon starts denaturing protein, mealy texture.
- **Boneless skinless thighs** cook ~25 min at 425°F. Bone-in skin-on take ~40 min (see other recipe). Don't confuse the two.

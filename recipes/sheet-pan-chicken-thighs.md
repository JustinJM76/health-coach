# Sheet-pan chicken thighs + sweet potato + broccoli

_First cooked: 2026-04-25 — confirmed family hit (cleaned the pan)_
_Serves: family of 4 + leftovers_

## Ingredients

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Bone-in skin-on chicken thighs | **3.5 lb (~6 large)** |
| Small sweet potatoes | 3, peeled, ½-inch cubes |
| Broccoli | 2–3 heads, cut into florets |
| Olive oil | 3–4 Tbsp |
| Kosher salt | |
| Black pepper | |
| Garlic powder | |
| Smoked paprika | |
| _Optional spicy:_ red pepper flakes, cayenne | |

## Method

1. **Preheat oven 425°F.** Line a large sheet pan with foil or parchment; have a second pan ready for broccoli.
2. **Sweet potatoes:** toss cubes with 1 Tbsp olive oil + ½ tsp salt + ¼ tsp pepper. Set aside.
3. **Chicken:** pat thighs *very* dry with paper towels (this is the difference between crispy and steamed skin). Rub with olive oil. Season top, bottom, and under skin: 1½ tsp salt, ¾ tsp pepper, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1½ tsp smoked paprika. **Spicy version:** add ½–1 tsp red pepper flakes + ¼–½ tsp cayenne to the rub.
4. **Chicken alone for 10 min.** Skin-up on the big sheet pan, into the oven.
5. **At 10 min:** add the sweet potato cubes around the chicken. Continue roasting.
6. **At 25 min total (i.e. 15 min after sweet potato joined):** prep broccoli — toss florets with 2 Tbsp olive oil + ½ tsp salt + ½ tsp garlic powder. Spread on the second pan and add to oven. **Plan 20–25 min for broccoli — this oven runs cool.**
7. **Check chicken at 35 min and again at 40 min total.** Target internal temp **175°F** at the thickest part. Pull at 175°F. **Do not push to 185°F+** — overcooks big thighs.
8. **Rest chicken 5 min** before serving. Everything else comes out at the same time.

## Plates

| Plate | Build |
|---|---|
| **Family** | 1–2 thighs (skin on), generous sweet potato, broccoli |
| **Justin** | 1 thigh **skin off** (~40 g protein), small/skip sweet potato, double broccoli, hot sauce |

## Why this works

- One-pan workflow (plus a second small pan for broccoli)
- Scales easily for family + leftovers
- Patting the chicken dry + roasting at 425°F gives crispy skin without deep frying
- Sweet potato + broccoli stagger cleanly so everything finishes together
- Spicy variant fits Justin's flavor preference; family-version (no cayenne) keeps it teen-friendly

## Iteration notes

### v1 — 2026-04-25 (first cook)
- **What I made:** 4 large thighs (~2.5 lb), 2 small sweet potatoes, 2 heads broccoli.
- **Result:** family devoured everything in one sitting. Wife wished there were leftovers for work lunch.
- **Lessons that got incorporated above:**
  - **Scale up.** 2.5 lb was not enough for this family. Plan 3.5 lb (~6 thighs) + 3 sweet potatoes going forward.
  - **Oven runs cool.** Broccoli needed 20–25 min, not the 15 originally planned. Adjust timing or oven calibration check.
  - **Pull chicken sooner.** First cook hit 185–190°F internal — slightly past optimal. Big thighs are forgiving but 175°F is the better target.
- **Justin's portion was right:** 1 skinless thigh + small sweet potato + heap of broccoli = comfortably full, ~40 g protein.

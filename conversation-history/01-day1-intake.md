# Day 1 — Tue 2026-04-21 — Intake

## Context entering the session
Justin set up `~/health` directory, configured Withings sync, exported labs PDF, ready for an intake conversation. Came in with a clear stated goal: 250 lb → 220 lb. Already practicing 1pm–8pm IF for ~1 year.

## What happened

**Profile capture.** Built `profile.md` from Justin's own input + lab review:
- 49M, 5'7", 250 lb (Withings 240.3 lb in October — has rebounded ~10 lb in 6 months).
- Blood pressure 111/73, fasting glucose 77, triglycerides 68 (excellent), HDL 60, TSH 1.7. Strong metabolic markers for his weight.
- Modestly elevated LDL (126), Non-HDL 142, Total cholesterol 202 — expected to improve with weight loss.
- PsA managed on Bimzelx. Joint flares in feet/knees relevant to exercise design.
- Cooks all family meals. Two athletically active teen daughters drive snack presence in the house.
- Self-diagnosed failure mode: ravenous at 1pm → oversized lunch + pre-prep grazing on pretzel chips & guac → afternoon drift snacking → daily surplus.
- History: Noom (-30 lb, 6 mo, burned out on tracking); Simple (dropped, felt designed for single adults); IF alone (-30, plateau, +15 rebound). **6-month tracking-burnout cliff is the recurring pattern.**

**Plan drafted (`plan.md`).** Three small experiments designed to address the failure mode without adding tracking burden:
1. **Pre-lunch protein primer** (~20g protein) before any kitchen prep; no pretzels during prep.
2. **One planned afternoon snack** (protein-forward), then kitchen closed.
3. **11:15pm bedtime anchor** on weeknights. Sleep was running ~5.5 hrs unfragmented.

Strategy commitments:
- No calorie counting, no macro targets. Track behaviors and trend, not arithmetic.
- One lever at a time. Three small experiments, not a 40-item overhaul.
- Social anchors (Friday Mexican lunch, weekend beers, Sunday burritos) untouched.
- Joints are a constraint — no high-impact, watch KB squat load.

**Equipment.** Justin ordered Bodylastics resistance band set (arrived Wed) and considered a 35 lb KB. We accelerated one program change for Wed: add **banded standing rows** (one pull movement) to address the push/quad-dominant routine.

**First day execution.**
- Workout 12:00–12:45 (3×10 KB squats @ 20 lb, pushups, glute bridges, 20 min elliptical).
- Primer at 1:20pm: **2 hard-boiled eggs** + Frank's Red Hot. No chips.
- Lunch ~1:35pm: ½ turkey sandwich (multigrain, mustard only, no mayo/cheese) + ½ can Progresso chicken & sausage gumbo. Reported "noticeably less ravenous than usual" going in, "almost a little too full" after.
- Coach signal: primer worked; lunch portion may need to dial down.
- 4pm snack: skipped (not hungry). Coach affirmed: snack is a tool against drift, not a mandate.
- Dinner ~6:45pm: 110 g salmon + 90 g cooked white rice + steamed broccoli pile + 2 small Pillsbury crescent rolls. "Comfortably full." Apple wasn't yet in the picture — crescent rolls flagged as the flex item to watch.
- Bed: Tuesday is BG3 game night with the boys. Plan: wrap by 10:30, shower, read, lights out 11:15.

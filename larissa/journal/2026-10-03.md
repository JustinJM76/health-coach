**The 10-min session (both days, same):**

Warmup (~2 min):
- Band pull-aparts × 10
- Arm circles × 10/direction

Movements (~7 min, 2 sets × 8 reps each — reduced volume from pre-pause 3×10):
1. **Banded row** — scap-first cue, elbows to ribs, 3-band stack as before (or less if too much)
2. **Inclined pushups** — hands elevated (countertop/chair back), scap-first before lowering
3. **Goblet squat with 20 lb KB** — moderate stance (adductor watch), narrow further if guarding pull

**Pain-monitoring rules (safety valve):**
- Shoulder pain in row/pushup → stop that movement, flag at next PT
- Adductor pain in squat → narrow stance / reduce depth; if still painful, skip squat
- Thoracic/cervical pain → stop session, report to PT
- OK: mild muscle soreness. Not OK: sharp pain, pulling sensations, joint discomfort

**Progression criteria:**
- Weeks 1–2: hold at 2×8 to establish tolerance
- If 2 clean weeks with no pain → progress to either 2×10 OR 3×8 (not both at once)
- Don't rush: 2 months paused + thoracic diagnosis + shoulder history + adductor still unaddressed = slow

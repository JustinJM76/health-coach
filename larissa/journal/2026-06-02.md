# 2026-06-02 — Sleep mask debrief, pivot to window blackout (session 4)

_21:56 EDT_

## Session

Short check-in. Larissa tried the sleep mask this morning. Reported back with a clean failure analysis. Pivoted the intervention.

## What happened with the mask

- Put on mask at sunrise → fell back asleep successfully (good).
- BUT: very groggy when alarm rang at 5:45; hit snooze multiple times; overslept by ~10 min; felt startled.
- Two specific failure modes she identified:
  1. **Conditioning:** years of using a mask only when she wants to sleep → mask = sleep cue. Made waking hard.
  2. **Alarm clock mismatch:** her alarm is a gradient-sunrise-light clock that she relies on for gentle waking. Mask blocked the gradient light along with the unwanted window light, so she lost the wake-up assist and got jolted by audio only.

Sharp self-diagnosis on her part.

## What I observed back

The mask solved early-light leak but blocked the right light source (alarm clock) along with the wrong one (window sun), and tripped her sleep conditioning. **Block at the source, not the receiver.**

## What was assigned

Pivot to **window blackout** in three progressive levels of investment, lowest first:

1. **Cheapest test:** tape cardboard / foam-core / doubled blackout trash bag over window for 2–3 nights. Answers "does eliminating window light fix it?" before spending money.
2. **Mid:** clip-on blackout liner for existing curtains (~$20–40).
3. **Full:** blackout curtains or inside-mount blackout shades.

Two questions to add to her log:
- Is the bedroom east-facing? (If yes, this is high-leverage.)
- On a blackout night, does the alarm clock's gradient still wake her well? (Success metric.)

The mask is shelved for home use, kept as a possible travel tool.

## Tone

Excellent debugging engagement — she came in with a structured failure report, not a vague "didn't work." Receptive to the pivot. Wrapping early.

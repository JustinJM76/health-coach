## New physiological understanding: late-luteal sleep fragmentation

Discussed with her the mechanism: estrogen + progesterone both drop in late luteal, cortisol naturally rises in second half of the night, together producing **vasomotor events that fragment sleep without necessarily producing a full "I'm hot and awake" moment.** Same underlying peri mechanism as the discrete warmth events, just presenting as unexplained wake-ups. 2nd-half-of-night pattern makes sense because REM (which dominates late sleep) is more vulnerable to hormonal disruption than deep sleep (which dominates early sleep).

## Weighted vest question — deferred

She owns a weighted vest but hasn't started using it due to prior neck/shoulder discomfort (now "almost completely" resolved). Asked whether to acclimate now by wearing during normal activity (not exercise yet).

**Answer: not yet — wait 4–6 weeks.** Rationale:

- The vest sits on traps/upper back — same body zone as the recent pushup shoulder signal AND her prior neck/shoulder discomfort. Adding vest load right now stacks a second new stressor onto tissues still adapting to RT.
- The RT she's doing IS the pre-work: banded rows + eventual full-range pushups directly strengthen the postural muscles that need to tolerate vest load.
- Vest is worth having on the roadmap: one of the most well-studied non-drug bone-density interventions; matters given maternal osteoporosis + peri.

**Reintroduction trigger** (she asked to be reminded): **(a) 3–4 consecutive RT sessions with no shoulder signal, AND (b) ~mid-August or later.** Body signal is the actual gate; date is the check-in prompt.

**When she does start:** lightest weight, 15–30 min at a time, once daily, normal activity only (not exercise). Build slowly — add 15 min every few days, then longer walks, then eventually exercise. Step back a stage if neck or shoulder flares.

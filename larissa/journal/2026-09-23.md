Larissa surfaced a body-signal she'd been noticing:

## Read

**Working hypothesis: unresolved right adductor strain from April 2026** (likely grade 1 based on pull-without-acute-tear signs). Healed at rest during summer break BUT never rehabilitated back to load-tolerance. Return to demanding stretching triggering protective guarding.

Mechanism cascade:
1. Scar tissue formed less elastic than original muscle
2. Muscle weakened in strained direction from disuse
3. Nervous system developed protective guarding reflex

**Her "strong pull as if I'm going to hurt myself" sensation IS the nervous system protecting her from re-injury.** That signal should be trusted, not overridden. Pushing through = real re-tear risk.

## What I told her

**Do:**
- Modify range in dance and yoga — work to edge of comfort, NOT edge of pull
- Warm up 3–5 min before class (walking, easy hip circles)
- Post-class: gentle movement, not aggressive stretching
- Contract-relax patterns safer than pulling into stretch

**Don't:**
- Aggressive stretching to "loosen it up" — wrong for post-strain tissue
- Compare to pre-April ROM — that's the target, not today's baseline

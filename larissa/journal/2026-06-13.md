## Late add — methodology correction (her catch)

After the main exchange, Larissa pointed out that she takes the electrolyte packets in the **afternoon**, so the right comparison for the magnesium-BM hypothesis is electrolyte day X → BM on day X+1, not same-day. She's right — magnesium's bowel effect typically has a 6–20 hour onset, so same-day comparison was misaligned.

Re-running with the correct lag:

**Electrolyte → next-day BM:**
- 6/6 yes → 6/7 NO BM (against)
- 6/9 yes → 6/10 regular ✓ (fits)
- 6/11 yes → 6/12 small (mixed)

**No-electrolyte → next-day BM:**
- 6/7 no → 6/8 regular (against in the other direction)
- 6/8 no → 6/9 very minimal
- 6/10 no → 6/11 small

**Honest correction-of-the-correction:** with the right time alignment, the data is **ambiguous, not opposite** as I framed it earlier. Sample is too small and noisy (period + other variables) to confirm or rule out. Magnesium hypothesis stays alive but unconfirmed. **No change to her electrolyte cadence** — next month's data will tell us more.

Good lesson on time-of-day in dosing comparisons. Worth carrying forward to any future "did X affect Y" log analysis.

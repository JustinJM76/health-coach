**Problem:** existing default is banana (~1g protein). Peri optimum is ~10–15g protein per snack for muscle protein synthesis efficiency.

**Experiment:** shift the default to something protein-forward. Two options:

- **Option A** (easiest): banana + 1 Tbsp natural peanut butter (~4g extra protein)

**Why:** small protein-forward snack blunts pre-dinner hunger; peri muscle protein synthesis benefits from meal-level protein density.

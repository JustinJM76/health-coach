## THE MAJOR INSIGHT — template eater

In the same message, Justin disclosed:

> "I'm a creature of habit. I ate the same lunch every school day from 2nd grade until the day I graduated high school...no variation. If we can develop some healthy routines, I should be able to stick to them. My schedule repeats on a weekly pattern, so if we can get to Monday is X, Tues Y... that would be super helpful for me."

This reframed the whole coaching approach.

**Coaching pivot:** instead of day-to-day flexible options, build a **Mon–Sun weekly template** with set slots. Rotate WITHIN slots (cottage cheese ↔ eggs ↔ Greek yogurt are all "primer slot"), not across slots. This matches his psychology AND is a defense against 6-month tracking burnout — because if the default IS the plan, there's nothing to track.

Profile and plan both updated to capture this.

**Coach asked Justin for a weekly schedule sketch** to draft template around — homework for later.

## Side topic — rotisserie chicken health

Justin asked: how healthy is supermarket rotisserie chicken? Coach:
- Salt yes (brine-injected, ~400–550 mg sodium per 3 oz; 2,500–3,000 mg total bird)
- Butter no (rendered chicken fat / vegetable oil)
- For Justin specifically (BP 111/73, optimizing for weight not sodium): **completely fine**
- One easy win: skip the skin — halves the fat, removes most surface brine concentrate

Tier ranking: Whole Foods clean-ingredient > standard supermarket > home-roast (best control but defeats purpose).

## Protein target — set explicitly

Justin asked: what's my daily protein target?

**Coach gave: 150–180 g/day** (1.6–1.8 g/kg of goal weight).

Reasoning:
- Cutting weight + lifting → protein protects lean mass during deficit
- Age 49 → anabolic resistance; older adults need more, not less
- Calculate against goal weight (100 kg / 220 lb), not current

**Estimated Justin's current intake: ~90–125 g/day** — under target by 30–60 g most days.

Levers to close gap without tracking:

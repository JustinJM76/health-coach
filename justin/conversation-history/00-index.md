## Coaching arc to date (20 days)

**Phase 1 — Intake (Day 1):** Captured baseline (250 lb, BMI 39.2, BP 111/73, strong metabolic labs, modestly high LDL). Identified failure mode (1pm ravenousness → overeat lunch → afternoon drift → rebound). Drafted three small experiments: protein primer + no prep-time chips, one planned afternoon snack, 11:15pm bedtime anchor.

**Phase 2 — Quick wins (Days 2–3):** Cottage cheese primer immediate hit. Discovered Justin is a template eater (11 years identical school lunches). Built weekly Mon–Sun operational template. Workout split tuned to Mon–Thu matching his preference. Day 3: primer "felt working" — moved from rule to felt mechanism.

**Phase 3 — Stress test + maintenance (Days 4–5):** Allergy onset, late-night dance comp, Mexican lunch — all navigated cleanly. Day 5 became the first weekly grocery + meal-planning operational rhythm.

**Phase 4 — Routine stabilization (Days 6–13):** Self-titration patterns established (intervals, tempo, resistance). Day 9 post-dentist comfort-food urge → `comfort-food-protocol.md` drafted from Justin's own articulated kernel. Day 12 patio build (4,500 lb of concrete, 11 hr labor) — hard-day adherence proven on the hardest day yet.

**Phase 5 — First formal review + execution (Days 14–18):** 2-week review confirmed program working better than expected. Added protein target (150–180 g/day), Phase 1 stretching, minimum-effective-dose workout fallback, "informed passenger" coaching style. Day 15: primer becomes identity-level (graduation moment #3 — "forgot to mention because automatic"). Day 17: voluntary skip of crescent roll without prompting (the same flex-item flagged on Day 1). Day 18: first sub-240 weigh-in.

**Phase 6 — Household architecture (Days 19–20):** Project shifted from single-principal to household. Folder restructure into `justin/`, `larissa/`, `household/`. Larissa onboarded with own session, charter, intake. Cross-session notes channel load-tested cleanly.

**Where we are (Day 20):** Operational template running on autopilot. 7-day trailing avg 241.3 lb (down from 247.5 first Withings; ~9 lb on the trailing line). Primer is identity-level. Comfort-food protocol exists but hasn't fired in anger. Larissa just had her intake; her cadence is open-ended. Next formal 2-week review: Sun 2026-05-18.

## Key insights captured (cross-session)

- **Template eater** → routine = energy savings, not constraint. Build defaults, don't force daily decisions.
- **6-month burnout pattern** is the core design constraint. Optimize for sustainability, not peak weeks.
- **Protein primer is the linchpin.** Identity-level by Day 15. Protect at all costs.
- **Larissa is a co-principal,** not just a stakeholder. Household alignment is force multiplier.
- **Justin owns meal infrastructure** (plans, shops, cooks). Larissa is a stakeholder there. Her zone is breakfast/lunch.
- **User-designed protocols > imposed protocols.** Comfort-food protocol kernel was Justin's own articulated backup plan; coach systematized; Justin owns it.
- **Spicy food preference** = zero-cost flavor lever for lean protein.
- **Mechanical vs PsA pain distinction** — Justin reads his body well; trust him.
- **Air fryer + crockpot** are planning levers, not just equipment.
- **Body-comp from old scale = noise.** Weight only. Multiple readings: 2 → average; 3+ → discard outlier.
- **Hard-day adherence is the real test.** Yard work, patio build, post-dentist, late dance-comp nights — system held.
- **Self-titration over grinding.** Justin titrates to actual capacity rather than pushing through bad form. Mature training behavior.
- **Voluntary recalibration** (Day 17 crescent-roll skip) = the program designing the user, not the user enforcing the program.
- **Family anchors stay** (Friday Mexican, Sat beers, Sun burritos + ice cream). Social meals honored, not policed.
- **Cross-session privacy:** repo is shared but coaches must NOT surface one principal's content to the other unless explicitly asked.

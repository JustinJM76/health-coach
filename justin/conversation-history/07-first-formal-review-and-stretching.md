# Days 14–18 (Mon 5/4 – Fri 5/8) — First formal 2-week review, primer becomes identity-level, sub-240 milestone

## Coaching arc

The structural review week. The 2-week formal review confirmed the program was working better than expected and surfaced the next-tier additions (protein target, stretching, science framing, minimum-effective-dose workout fallback). Then a few days of execution that produced two milestones: the **primer becoming automatic** (graduation moment #3) and the **first sub-240 weigh-in.**

## Patterns filed
- **Habit graduation arc** is a real coaching milestone, not a coach abstraction. Three discrete moments documented.
- **Voluntary flex-item recalibration** (Day 17) without prompting = the program designing the user, not the user enforcing the program.
- **Self-regulation hierarchy under pressure:** when something has to give, the order is *stretches > snacks > workout > primer.* Justin held this hierarchy clean across multiple stressful days.
- **Social-anchor flex (3 beers Friday)** ≠ a slip; **martyr-meal abstinence** ≠ virtue. Neither extreme is the program.

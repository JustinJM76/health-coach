## Autophagy / extended fast discussion (Day 27)

Justin asked about occasional OMAD (skipping lunch ~1–2×/week) for autophagy. Coach response: human evidence for "16-hour autophagy trigger" is weaker than the podcast discourse suggests (most claims extrapolated from mouse + in-vitro studies; direct human evidence of meaningful autophagy upregulation is at 48h+, not 16–24h). He's already getting baseline autophagy at sustained 17:7. The marginal upside is uncertain; the costs are concrete:
- Protein/muscle protection during deficit (he's already under 150–180g target most days)
- Workout fueling (he trains fasted before lunch — OMAD means skip the workout or train very deep-fasted)
- System complexity vs. template-eater principle

**Decision: defer, revisit at 220 / maintenance / Phase 2 decision point.** Not contraindicated; just wrong risk/reward in active deficit. Justin agreed.

## "Friday flex" pattern — added to coaching playbook
When the family's preferred meal isn't on-program, Justin can:
1. Cook the family-favorite without modification
2. Eat a parallel option for himself (leftovers, salad with leftover protein, etc.)
3. Not make the family eat his version, not make himself eat theirs

Avoids two failure modes: (a) martyring through a high-calorie meal because "it's what's served," (b) sterilizing the kitchen and creating family friction.
